Getting started

  1. Unzip the whole folder, then double-click RelicProtocol.exe.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the alpha isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Press Play online. The test server is already filled in: type your name, press Connect, pick a hero and press Ready. Bots fill empty seats.
  4. If the game says your version is out of date, come back here for the newest build.

Controls

Right-click to move and attack, A to attack-move, Q W E R to aim an ability and left-click to cast, Ctrl+Q/W/E/R to learn one, B to recall, Tab for the scoreboard, Space to centre the camera.
